# catandcapy.com

Astro + Svelte site for Cat & Capy. See README.md for structure and commands; pushing to `main` deploys.

## Writing on the site

- Keep text short. Many visitors are kids and young teens coming from TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.
- Show things with images, icons and stickers instead of explaining them in sentences.
- Use the shortest wording that works: "iOS soon", not "The iPhone version is coming soon".
- Don't promise what the user can't guarantee. Newsletter subscribers get a *chance* to play games early, never a promise.
